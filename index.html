Technical Notes

Frequently asked questions

Matte PLA+ is ideal for indoor residential pendants and table lamps, offering crisp surface definition and soft parchment diffusion. PETG adds higher moisture and impact resilience for coastal cities or humid spaces. High-temp ABS is engineered for commercial hospitality zones or enclosed fixtures requiring elevated thermal stability up to 95°C.

Yes. Beyond our 8 studio finishes, we formulate custom filament colour matches for architectural palettes starting at 5 units (or single bespoke commissions with a minor setup fee). Share your RAL or Pantone code via our inquiry form or WhatsApp.

All lumarq.3d fixtures are pre-wired for Indian 230V AC supply using CE/BIS-certified E27 brass holders, 1.8m fabric-braided cord (or in-line rotary dimmer switch on table/floor lamps), and a matching matte ceiling canopy. Please use LED bulbs only (max 15W LED, warm 2700K–3000K recommended; bulb included on select series).

Each luminaire is printed fresh to your selected finish and scale in 4–6 business days, inspected for thermal and dimensional consistency, and dispatched in custom protective cradling across India.
